Northwind Analytics Inc. — FY2025 Internal Briefing
Confidential — for ClarityAI document-upload testing only

Company overview
Northwind Analytics Inc. is a privately held software company headquartered in Austin, Texas.
It sells a cloud analytics platform used by mid-market retailers and logistics firms.
Founded in 2017 by Maya Chen and Omar Haddad. Employees: 412 as of 31 March 2025.

Financial highlights (fiscal year ended 31 March 2025)
- Annual revenue: $86.4 million (up 28% year over year)
- Recurring revenue (ARR): $79.1 million
- Gross margin: 71%
- Operating loss: $4.2 million (improved from $11.8 million loss in FY2024)
- Cash and equivalents: $31.5 million
- Remaining performance obligations (RPO): $52.0 million

Product
The flagship product is Northwind Pulse, a warehouse-native analytics suite.
In January 2025 the company launched Pulse Forecast, a demand-planning add-on.
Pulse Forecast contributed $6.8 million of new ARR in its first quarter.

Customers and market
- 1,140 paying customers at year end
- Largest customer: Harborline Logistics (8% of revenue)
- Net revenue retention: 118%
- Primary competitors: Tableau, Power BI, and Looker

Risks called out in this briefing
- Customer concentration: top 10 accounts are 34% of revenue
- Pulse Forecast is not yet profitable on a contribution-margin basis
- Two open SOC 2 Type II findings related to access reviews (target close: Q2 FY2026)

Guidance for FY2026
Management expects revenue of $108–$112 million and ARR of about $100 million
if Pulse Forecast attach rates stay above 22%.
